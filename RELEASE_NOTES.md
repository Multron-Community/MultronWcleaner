## Multron Win Cleaner 1.29.6 Update

### Fixes
- **No more repeated error during Malware Scan Cloud.** When a malware scan was stopped or finished while files were still being uploaded, those files were added to the activity list twice. The list then failed every second with *Index must be within the bounds of the List* and the error message kept coming back. Files that finish uploading after the scan has stopped are no longer added again, and the list can no longer fail on a duplicate entry.

### Updating
- **From 1.29.5 or earlier:** the app updates itself to 1.29.6 as usual (Settings > App Updates).
- **New installs:** download `mwc_setup.exe` (installer) or `mwc.zip` (portable) below.
